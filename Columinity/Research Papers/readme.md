# RESEARCH PAPERS

The research papers in this directory were performed based on anonymized data from [Columinity](https://columinity.com) and other sources. The research is made possible by [The Liberators Improve Your Team BV](https://columinity.com) in collaboration with the Lucid Institute for Organizational Science (LIOS)

The studies are performed by a group of researchers, Christiaan Verwijs, Prof. Daniel Russo, Prof. Karen Eilers, Evelien Roos and others.

Unless otherwise specified, all papers are peer-reviewed and published in academic journals.W
