# THE LIBERATORS - FACILITATION MATERIALS

The purpose of [The Liberators](https://github.com/theliberators/facilitation-materials/blob/production/Assets/logo.png) (Barry Overeem and Christiaan Verwijs) is to liberate teams from ineffective ways of organizing work by giving them control over shaping their future. Since 2019, with the help of sponsors, we have produced tons of materials to help you improve work in your organization. This includes exercise materials, do-it-yourself workshops, posters, and kits. We make this material available here, free of charge.

We wish to thank all the sponsors - our patrons, customers - who helped us create these materials.
