# THE LIBERATORS - FACILITATION MATERIALS

The purpose of [The Liberators](https://theliberators.com/) (Barry Overeem and Christiaan Verwijs) is to humanize the workplace and unleash organizational superpowers. Since 2019, and with the help of sponsors, we have produced tons of materials to help you humanize work in your organization. This includes exercise materials, do-it-yourself workshops, posters, and kits. We make this material available here, free of charge.
